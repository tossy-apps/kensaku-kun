High-Fidelity Cell Rendering

Accurately renders fill colors, font styles, borders, text orientation, strikethroughs, and merged cell regions. Instantly understand the context without launching Microsoft Excel.

Shapes & Comments Extraction

Scans texts inside flowcharts, text boxes, sticky notes, and cell comments. Renders shape colors and outlines right on top of the preview grid.
